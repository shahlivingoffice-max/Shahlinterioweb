About Shah Living LLP

Steel Furniture | Stronger Spaces | Brighter Tomorrows
Advanced Machinery Precision Manufacturing Rust Resistant Finish Modern Designs Bulk Production B2B Specialists

At Shah Living LLP, we combine advanced manufacturing technology with skilled craftsmanship to create durable, functional, and aesthetically refined steel furniture and industrial solutions.

Built on the principles of precision, quality, and innovation, our manufacturing facility is equipped with state-of-the-art CNC laser cutting machines, CNC press brakes, and modern fabrication systems that enable us to deliver products with exceptional accuracy and consistency.

From office storage systems, wardrobes, almirahs, lockers, and industrial furniture to customized metal fabrication projects, every product is engineered to meet the highest standards of strength, performance, and longevity.

FAQ

Questions Buyers Ask Us

Straight answers about the brand, the steel, and how to order.

What is Securex?
Securex is a steel furniture brand manufactured by Shah Interio (registered as Shah Living LLP) in Kantabanji, Balangir district, Odisha, India. The firm was founded in 1985 and sells factory-direct to B2B buyers.
What are Securex almirahs made of?
Securex almirahs are built from CRCA (cold-rolled close-annealed) steel sheet with reinforced joints, finished with a rust-resistant powder coating. Glossy-finish and printed-panel options are produced in-house.
What products does Securex manufacture?
Securex manufactures six categories of steel furniture: home almirahs, office almirahs, dressing almirahs, lockers, bookshelves and glass showcases.
Does Securex accept bulk and B2B orders?
Yes. Securex supplies wholesalers, retailers and institutional buyers factory-direct with flexible minimum order quantities. Because there is no middleman, bulk pricing is quoted straight from the manufacturing unit.
Can Securex almirahs be customised?
Yes. Dimensions, colours, locking systems and internal layouts can all be built to specification. Customisation is available on bulk and institutional orders.
Where is Securex steel furniture manufactured?
At the Shah Interio unit in Kantabanji, Balangir district, Odisha, India (PIN 767039). The plant runs fiber laser cutting machines, CNC bending machines, almirah printing machines and glossy finishing units. Securex supplies across Odisha and the rest of India.
How do I get a Securex price list or quote?
Call or message +91 9179539610 on WhatsApp, or send an enquiry from shahinterio.com. Quotes for bulk and custom orders are normally returned the same day.
