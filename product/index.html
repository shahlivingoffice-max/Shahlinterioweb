About the Securex catalog

Securex is the steel furniture brand of Shah Interio, registered as Shah Living LLP and manufacturing since 1985 at Brajesh Steel (Parent Company of Shah Living LLP), Kantabanji, Balangir district, Odisha 767039, India.

Every product in this catalog is fabricated from CRCA (cold-rolled close-annealed) steel sheet with reinforced joints and a rust-resistant powder-coated finish. Glossy-finish and printed-panel variants are produced in-house on fiber laser cutting machines, CNC bending machines, almirah printing machines and glossy finishing units.

The range covers home almirahs, office almirahs, dressing almirahs, lockers, bookshelves and glass showcases. All pricing is factory-direct B2B with flexible minimum order quantities, and dimensions, colours, locking systems and internal layouts can be customised on bulk and institutional orders.
